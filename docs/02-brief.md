# 02 — Messaging Brief (research-consolidator)

## 1. ICP
Founders, COOs, and department heads at **20–1,000 person** companies (services, logistics, SaaS,
healthcare admin, finance ops). They've tried ChatGPT or a pilot; nothing reached production.
**Trigger:** board/CEO asks for an AI plan, or workload grows faster than headcount.

## 2. Top 3 pains (their words)
1. "We tried AI and nobody actually uses it." (failed pilots, zero adoption)
2. "My team is drowning in manual, copy-paste work." (cost + burnout)
3. "Every vendor says AI, nobody shows ROI." (distrust, no clear starting point)

## 3. Desired outcome (90 days)
One or two AI workflows **live in production**, used daily, with a measured result (hours saved,
cost per ticket down, faster turnaround), plus a roadmap for what's next.

## 4. Core promise
**Get your first AI workflow live and paying for itself in 30 days — built, adopted, and measured.**

## 5. Unique mechanism — "The 30-Day ROI Sprint"
Find → Build → Adopt → Measure. We pick *one* high-ROI workflow, build it inside your existing tools
and cloud, train the team, and track the number. Removes pain #1 (adoption is part of the scope),
#2 (targets the most manual workflow first), #3 (ROI is measured, not promised).
*Chosen over a "strategy-first" angle because buyers explicitly say they don't want another deck.*

## 6. Offer stack
- **Free:** 30-min AI Opportunity Call → you receive a written **AI Opportunity Map** (top 3 use cases, effort, est. ROI) within 48h.
- **AI Opportunity Sprint** (2 weeks) — audit workflows, rank use cases, ROI model, roadmap.
- **30-Day Build Sprint** — ship one production AI workflow (agent, copilot, automation) + training.
- **AI Team Enablement** — workshops, usage policy, prompt playbooks, champion program.
- **Fractional AI Lead** (monthly) — ongoing roadmap, builds, monitoring, vendor guidance.

## 7. Risk reversal
Free call + written map whether or not you hire us. Build Sprint: if we don't hit the agreed success
metric, **we keep working at no extra cost until we do**. NDA by default; your data stays in your environment.
*(User must confirm they will honour this guarantee.)*

## 8. Objections → answers (ranked)
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|

| Too expensive | Fixed-scope sprints with price up front; first workflow is chosen to pay back fast. |

| No internal time | ~2 hrs/week from one owner; we do the build. |
| Sales pitch fear | Call = diagnosis + written map; no deck. |
| Adoption | Training, champions, and usage tracked as a success metric. |
| Maintenance | Handover docs + optional Fractional AI Lead. |
| Just ChatGPT resellers? | Model-agnostic; we pick the right model/tool per workflow. |

## 10. Voice & tone
1. Plain-spoken operator, not "AI guru". 2. Specific numbers & timeframes. 3. Short sentences.
4. Confident, never hype. 5. "You" > "we".
**Banned:** revolutionize, cutting-edge, leverage, synergy, unlock, seamless, game-changer, harness, journey, next-gen.
