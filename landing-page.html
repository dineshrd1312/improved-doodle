FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

The call and Opportunity Map are free. Sprints are fixed-price and quoted up front; most first projects land between $8k and $25k depending on scope. You'll know the number before anything starts.

We tried AI and it flopped. Why would this be different?

Most pilots fail on integration and adoption, not the model. Both are part of our scope, and usage is one of the success metrics we track.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We work under NDA, build inside your own cloud and accounts where possible, use providers that don't train on your data, and apply role-based access.

How much of my team's time does it take?

About 2 hours a week from one internal owner, plus a short training session for users. We do the heavy lifting.

What if it doesn't deliver?

We agree on a success metric before we start. If the Build Sprint doesn't hit it, we keep working at no extra cost until it does.

Do you only use ChatGPT?

No. We're model-agnostic and pick the right model and tools for each workflow — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, open-source, or plain automation when AI isn't needed.

Who maintains it after launch?

You get full handover docs and training. Many clients keep us on as a Fractional AI Lead for monitoring and the next builds.

Is my company too small for this?

If your team spends 10+ hours a week on repetitive work, there's almost certainly a workflow worth automating. The free call will tell you honestly.
